🏛️ CGST + SGST vs IGST — fark kya hai?

GST ek hi tax hai, bas jama kahan hota hai ye sale ki jagah par depend karta hai:

  • Same state (Intra-state): GST aadha Centre ko (CGST) + aadha State ko (SGST). 18% = 9% + 9%.
  • Dusri state (Inter-state): Poora GST IGST ke roop me Centre ko jata hai. 18% = 18% IGST.
  • Total GST dono case me same rehta hai — sirf breakup badalta hai.

📐 Formula yaad rakho

GST jodna hai (Exclusive):

  • GST = Amount × Rate ÷ 100
  • Total = Amount + GST

GST nikalna hai (Inclusive):

  • Base = Price × 100 ÷ (100 + Rate)
  • GST = Price − Base

Example: ₹11,800 ki cheez @18% inclusive → Base = 11,800 × 100 ÷ 118 = ₹10,000, GST = ₹1,800.

GST slabs — kis par kitna?

India me GST ke mukhya slabs 5%, 12%, 18% aur 28% hain. 18% sabse common hai — zyadatar services aur bahut se goods isi par aate hain. Apne product/service ka exact slab hamesha GST rate schedule / apne CA se confirm karo, kyuki rates GST Council ke faislo se badal sakte hain.

Aksar puche jaane wale sawaal

GST inclusive price se base amount kaise nikalein?

Price ko (100 + rate) se divide karke 100 se multiply karo. Jaise ₹1,180 @18% inclusive hai to base = 1,180 × 100 ÷ 118 = ₹1,000 aur GST = ₹180. Upar calculator me “Remove GST” mode yahi karta hai.

Invoice par CGST/SGST alag-alag dikhana zaroori hai?

Haan, same-state sale ke invoice par CGST aur SGST alag lines me dikhaye jate hain, aur inter-state sale par IGST ek line me. Isi liye ye calculator dono breakup deta hai taaki bill copy karna asaan ho.

Kya main apna custom GST rate use kar sakta hoon?

Haan — slab ke neeche custom rate box me koi bhi rate (jaise 0% ya koi special rate) daalo, calculation usi par ho jayega.

Ye calculator kitna sahi hai?

Calculation standard GST formula par hai aur paise 2 decimal tak round hote hain. Filing ya bade invoice se pehle apne accountant se final figure confirm kar lo — ye tool estimate ke liye hai, tax advice nahi.
